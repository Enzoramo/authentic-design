# Sources and scope

These references support usability, accessibility, process, and packaging. Originality probes and design records are proposed working methods, not scientifically validated measures. No source establishes that this skill is the best or guarantees unique output.

- [Nielsen Norman Group: 10 usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) — broad usability principles for feedback, recovery, terminology, and consistency; not a substitute for research.
- [Design Council: Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/) — understanding problems, considering alternatives, and testing solutions. The process is adapted to task size; no source text or diagram is bundled.
- [W3C: How to Meet WCAG 2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) — applicable criteria; a short checklist is not conformance.
- [W3C: Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [Target Size Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html), and [Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) — thresholds, exceptions, and explanations.
- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines) — consult current guidance for relevant Apple platforms; this skill does not replace it.
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) — portable `SKILL.md` packaging. Hosts determine installation, invocation, discovery, and tool access.

Core web and process references checked on 2026-11-12. Platform guidance remains a task-time reference. Third-party sources and assets retain their licenses; this repository's MIT license applies to its original material.
